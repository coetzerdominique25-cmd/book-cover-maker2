Yes. Set Copies to 2, 3 or 4 for subjects with more than one book, and each copy is added to the PDF.

Does the History cover show my child's grade?

Yes, the name, grade and (optionally) school and year are printed on every cover. Grades up to 4 characters work, for example 8, 8B or 12A.
